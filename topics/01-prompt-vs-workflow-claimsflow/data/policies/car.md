# Meridian Mutual — Private Car Comprehensive Policy (wording CAR-2026)

Meridian Mutual is a made-up insurer. This wording exists only for testing.

## 1. What this policy covers

We pay for physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by:
- a collision with another vehicle, an animal, or a fixed object (a wall, a pole, a barrier)
- the vehicle being hit while parked
- hail, flood or fallen trees
- fire, or attempted theft

Bodily injury is not covered by this policy. It is handled under the compulsory motor insurance.

## 2. Who may drive

The vehicle is covered only when the driver at the time of the event:
- is named as a listed driver in the policy schedule, and
- is at least the minimum driver age shown in the schedule on the day of the event.

## 3. Policy period

We cover events that happen between the start date and the end date in the schedule, both days included. An event outside these dates is not covered, even if the claim is sent while a policy is active.

## 4. Exclusions

We do not pay for:
- damage while the vehicle was driven by a person who is not a listed driver
- damage while the driver was younger than the minimum driver age
- damage while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- racing, speed tests or off-road driving
- wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown

## 5. How the amount is set

- The recognized amount is the approved repair cost in the **assessor's report**. Quotes from garages do not replace the assessor's report.
- We deduct the **deductible** shown in the schedule from every approved claim.
- If the recognized amount is lower than the deductible, we pay nothing.

## 6. Documents you must send

1. A signed claim form
2. The driver's license of the person who drove at the time of the event
3. Photos of the damage
4. The assessor's report

The documents must belong to this event and to the people named in the claim. If a document is missing, or it belongs to another person or another event, we ask for it before we decide.
