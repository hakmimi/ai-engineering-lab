Claims Intake Flow

One claim, three steps. Each step uses one workflow pattern. Car comprehensive, home water damage and travel claims, all made up.

Three steps

1 · Route

What kind of claim?

Sort it: car, water damage or travel. Each type gets its own document list and rules.

2 · Parallel

Check all documents at once

For each required document: is it there, and is it the right one? Missing or wrong stops the claim.

3 · Chain

Decide step by step

Covered? Any exclusion? How much? The AI decides, plain code does the math.

What each claim type checks

Car · comprehensive

Required documents
  • Claim form
  • Driver's license
  • Photos of the damage
  • Assessor's report
Typical exclusions
  • Driver not listed, or under the agreed age
  • Policy not active on the date
repair 12,000 ₪ − deductible 1,500 ₪
= pays 10,500 ₪

Home · water damage

Required documents
  • Claim form
  • Photos of the damage
  • Plumber's finding (water source)
  • Repair quote
Typical exclusions
  • No water-damage cover bought
  • Pipe wear or corrosion
quote 8,000 ₪ − deductible 1,000 ₪
= pays 7,000 ₪

Travel · medical or cancellation

Required documents
  • Claim form
  • Medical summary, or proof of the cancel reason
  • Receipts, or supplier refund letters
Typical exclusions
  • Event outside the trip dates
  • Reason not on the covered list
paid 10,000 ₪ − refunds 3,000 ₪ = 7,000 ₪
cap 5,000 ₪ → pays 5,000 ₪
