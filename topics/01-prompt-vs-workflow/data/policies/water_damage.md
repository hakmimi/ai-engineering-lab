# Meridian Mutual — Home Policy, Water Damage Section (wording HOME-2026)

Meridian Mutual is a made-up insurer. This wording exists only for testing.

## 1. What this section covers

This section applies only if the **water_damage** add-on appears in the policy schedule.

We pay for damage to the insured home (walls, floors, ceilings, built-in kitchen and cabinets) caused by a **sudden** escape of water from:
- a water or drainage pipe inside the insured home
- a household appliance connected to the water supply (washing machine, dishwasher, water heater)

The cost of finding the leak and repairing the pipe is included when the escape of water is covered.

## 2. Policy period

We cover events that are discovered between the start date and the end date in the schedule, both days included.

## 3. Exclusions

We do not pay for:
- damage from water that leaked **slowly over a long period** because of wear, corrosion, rust or rot of the pipe
- damage from water that came from a neighbor's home or from shared building pipes (claim from the neighbor or the building)
- damage from rain entering through an open window or a roof
- any water damage when the policy does not include the water_damage add-on

## 4. Repair route and how the amount is set

- **Network plumber:** if the repair is done by a plumber from the Meridian network, we pay 100% of the recognized amount.
- **Private plumber:** if the customer uses a plumber outside the Meridian network, we pay the **private rate** shown in the schedule (usually 90%) of the recognized amount.
- The recognized amount is the total in the **assessor's report** when there is one. If there is no assessor's report, it is the total in the **repair quote**.
- We deduct the **deductible** shown in the schedule.

## 5. Documents you must send

1. A signed claim form
2. Photos of the damage
3. The plumber's finding: where the water came from and why
4. A repair quote

The documents must belong to this event and to the insured home. If a document is missing, or it belongs to another person or another address, we ask for it before we decide. An assessor's report, when we send an assessor, is an extra document and is not required from the customer.
