# Meridian Mutual — Travel Insurance Policy (wording TRAVEL-2026)

Meridian Mutual is a made-up insurer. This wording exists only for testing.

The policy period is the trip: from the start date to the end date in the schedule, both days included. Part B (trip cancellation) also covers the time between buying the policy and the start date, because a trip is cancelled before it begins.

## Part A — Medical expenses abroad

### A1. What is covered

We pay for emergency medical treatment the insured receives **abroad, during the policy period**: doctor visits, medicines on prescription, emergency room and hospital stay.

### A2. Exclusions

We do not pay for:
- treatment that took place before the start date or after the end date
- treatment of a medical condition the insured had before the trip (a pre-existing condition), unless the **pre_existing** add-on appears in the schedule
- treatment that was planned before the trip, or cosmetic treatment
- treatment of a person who is not insured under this policy

### A3. How the amount is set

- The recognized amount is the total of the **receipts** issued to the insured.
- **Network provider:** clinics and hospitals in the Meridian network bill us directly or are refunded at 100%.
- **Private provider:** a clinic or hospital outside the Meridian network is refunded at the **private rate** shown in the schedule.
- The medical cap in the schedule is the most we pay per trip. The deductible in the schedule is deducted.

### A4. Documents you must send

1. A signed claim form
2. A medical summary from the treating doctor, with the diagnosis and the date
3. Receipts issued in the insured's name

## Part B — Trip cancellation

### B1. What is covered

We pay the money the insured paid in advance for the trip and cannot get back, if the trip is cancelled before departure for one of these reasons only:
- sudden illness or injury of the insured or a close family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling), confirmed by a doctor
- death of a close family member
- a reserve duty call-up order received after the trip was booked
- serious damage to the insured's home in the week before departure

### B2. Not covered

Any other reason is not covered, for example: a work meeting, a change of plans, fear of flying, a cheaper deal elsewhere.

### B3. How the amount is set

- The recognized amount is **what was paid in advance minus what the suppliers (airline, hotel, agency) refunded**.
- The cancellation cap in the schedule is the most we pay. The deductible in the schedule is deducted.

### B4. Documents you must send

1. A signed claim form
2. Proof of the reason for cancelling (for example a medical certificate or a call-up order)
3. Receipts for what was paid in advance
4. Letters from the suppliers showing what they refunded (or that they refunded nothing)
